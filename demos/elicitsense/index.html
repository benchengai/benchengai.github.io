Meeting recordings in, a requirements register out.

ElicitSense turns requirements-elicitation meetings into a register an analyst can review: every requirement cites the exact words and moment it came from, hesitant statements are flagged for confirmation, changes are tracked across meetings, and questions are answered with checked citations.

Grounded in research

Built from the systematic mapping study Emotions in Software Engineering (57 studies) and the multimodal elicitation prototype MEmoRE (IEEE RE 2023). Requirements engineering is among the least-studied phases for emotion research, and no study in the map evaluates a tool that supports stakeholder communication as it happens.

Cues, not emotion labels

ElicitSense reports observable cues (hedging, fillers, pauses, slow speech) on a requirement, never an emotion and never a score for a person. Cues are off for internal team meetings, because the EU AI Act prohibits AI emotion inference in the workplace.

Demo mode: every requirement, link, answer and score on this site was produced by a real run of the ElicitSense pipeline on synthetic, fictional meetings. Nothing here was written by hand to look like model output.
